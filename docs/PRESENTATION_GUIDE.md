# Presentation Guide

Two of your deliverables are a slide deck and a demo video. This guide keeps them focused; it is not scored on production polish.

# Slide Deck

A concise deck (≤30 slides) covering:

1. **The problem and your tool** — what it does and for whom.
1. **Scenarios addressed** — which of the five benchmark scenarios, and why.
1. **How it works** — the methodology at a level a policymaker can follow.
1. **Key findings** — your scenario ranking and the main drivers.
1. **Assumptions and limitations** — what matters most and what the tool cannot say.
1. **How to use it** — who runs it and how.

Export the final deck to PDF so reviewers can open it without your software.

# Demo Video

A **5-minute** screen recording of the tool in use. Aim to show:

- The tool running on real inputs (not slides about the tool).
- At least one scenario comparison from start to result.
- Where a user changes inputs and what changes in the output.

Keep it unedited or lightly edited; clarity matters more than production value. Host it somewhere reviewers can view without an account, and put the link in your submission.

# Tips

- Lead with the result, then explain the method.
- Show one scenario end-to-end rather than rushing through all five.
- Name your biggest assumption out loud — reviewers reward honesty.
